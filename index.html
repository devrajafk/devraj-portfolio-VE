Workflow
How I work
01

Story

Every project starts with understanding the story, emotion, and overall vision. This helps me create visuals that truly support the narrative.

02

Script

I turn ideas into clear scene-by-scene scripts with proper flow and pacing. This becomes the foundation for the entire project.

03

Shot Breakdown

Each scene is planned shot by shot with camera angles, framing, and movement. It makes the production process much smoother.

04

Character Design

I design detailed characters with a consistent look, outfit, and personality. This helps maintain continuity throughout the project.

05

Environment Design

I create locations and backgrounds that match the story's mood and style. Every environment is designed to feel immersive and believable.

10

Final Delivery

After the final review, I deliver the project in high quality and ready for use. Every detail is checked before delivery.

09

Editing

I put everything together with clean editing, smooth pacing, Suitable Tranistions, and color grading. This is where the story comes together.

08

Image-to-Motion / Prompt-to-motion

I create smooth, cinematic videos from both images and text prompts. The goal is to make every shot feel cinematic.

07

AI Generation

Using the right AI tools, I generate visuals that match the planned style and quality. I keep improving the outputs until they're production-ready.

06

Prompt Engineering

I write detailed prompts that guide the AI to produce the exact look and feel I want. Every prompt is refined until the results are consistent.
